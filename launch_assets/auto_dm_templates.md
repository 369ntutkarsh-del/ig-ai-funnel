- **Public comment auto-replies (rotate these):**
  1. `Sent to your DMs! Check requests`
  2. `Just dropped the direct link in your inbox`
  3. `Check your DM—sent the full list!`
- **Optional follow-gate prompt (ReplyKaro Free):**

  `Hey! Tap Follow first so Instagram lets me send links cleanly, then hit the button below.`

- **Automated DM message:**

  `Here's the exact link from the Reel: [LIVE_LINK_HUB_URL] — I also pinned my full 2026 100+ Free AI Tools, Prompts & Automation Pack at the top if you want everything organized in one place.`

Replace `[LIVE_LINK_HUB_URL]` before configuring the rule. Set up only through the provider's official Meta OAuth flow; do not share Instagram or Facebook passwords with any provider.
